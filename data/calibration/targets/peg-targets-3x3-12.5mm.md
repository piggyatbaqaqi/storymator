# peg-targets-3x3-12.5mm.png

Four ChArUco targets to cut out and glue to the animation disc, at
N, E and W of the paper with the fourth spare or south of the bar.

Generated by `bin/make-peg-targets`, which is where the parameters
live. Regenerate rather than edit.

| | |
|---|---|
| boards | 4, of 3 x 3 squares |
| square | 12.5 mm |
| marker | 9.0 mm, `DICT_6X6_250` |
| ids | #0 `0-3`, #1 `4-7`, #2 `8-11`, #3 `12-15` |
| board size | 37.5 mm a side |
| sheet | 215.9 x 279.4 mm at 508 dpi |

**The ids are disjoint on purpose.** Each piece is self-identifying,
so a partial view still contributes and no two pieces can be confused
once they are on the disc.

**Cut on the grey box, not on the board.** The box sits 8 mm outside
the squares. The four chessboard corners each piece contributes are
*interior* — a cut along the board's own boundary would not harm them,
but a cut a millimetre inside it destroys the contrast a corner is
made of.

**Measure the scale bar after printing and record what you get.** The
7 x 9 board came out 1.27 % oversize with a 0.15 % anisotropy between
its axes, and this print will have its own error. A 1 % error here is
a millimetre on the bar, which a caliper reads without argument.
