# 19.5" Rotating Animation Desk & Projector Housing Manual

This blueprint guide outlines the steps required to purchase materials, cut components, and assemble a 45-degree tilting drafting table equipped with a rotating 18-inch inner core housing an **AAXA M8 UST projector**. 

---

## 🛠️ Hardware Store Sourcing List

### 🌲 Aisle: Specialty Wood & Project Panels
*   **3/4-inch Plywood Project Panels (Birch or Sande):** Buy two **2ft x 4ft pre-cut boards**. Do not buy rough framing lumber. 
*   **1/2-inch Plywood Project Panel (Birch or Sande):** Buy one **2ft x 4ft pre-cut board**.
*   *Sourcing Note:* Check the specialty project bins at the **Home Depot on Oxford Drive** first. If 1/2-inch project sheets are out of stock, use **Lowe's on Baptist Rd** as your alternative destination.

### ⚙️ Aisle: Hardware & Fasteners
*   **16-inch Aluminum Heavy-Duty Lazy Susan Bearing:** (e.g., Besimple Heavy Duty Ring).
*   **Inline Skate Wheels / Rigid Roller Caster Bearings:** Three (3) total. Standard 2-inch to 3-inch profile wheel diameter.
*   **Axle Bolts:** Three (3) Grade 8 machine bolts matching your wheel hub diameter, along with matching hex nuts, locking nylon nuts, and broad metal fender washers.
*   **Structural Wood Screws:** One pack of 1-1/4 inch screws (for the 1/2" shelf) and one pack of 1-5/8 inch wood screws (for 3/4" plate fastening).
*   **Wood Glue:** Premium Titebond II or equivalent structural wood adhesive.

### ⚡ Aisle: Electrical & Building Supplies
*   **18-inch Diameter Cardboard Sonotube:** Procure from **Options Supply** (located near your daughter's apartment). Bring a heavy-duty utility knife or drywall saw to cut the 12-foot stock tube down to a manageable **14-inch segment** in the parking lot before loading it into your vehicle.
*   **Flat Extension Cord:** Low-profile style to lay flat against the central rotational base.
*   **Cable Ties / P-Clips:** For structural stress-relief anchoring near the projector chassis ports.

---

## 📐 Cutting Guide & Dimensions

### 3/4-Inch Plywood Cut Layout
1.  **Static Desk Base Plate:** Cut one square exactly **22.0" x 22.0"**. Drill an internal circle of **14.5"** right in the middle to permit cable routing and heat ventilation drops.
2.  **Outrigger Arms:** Cut three identical rectangles measuring **3.0" wide by 14.0" tall**. Drill an 8mm or 5/16" hole through the flat width centered at **11.0"** high to serve as your skate wheel axle.
3.  **Cylinder Base Plug:** Cut a precise circular disc with an outer diameter of **17.5"** (to snugly slide up inside the 18" Sonotube's 1/4" walls). Hollow out the exact center with a **14.5"** circular cutout.

### 1/2-Inch Plywood Cut Layout
1.  **Projector Mounting Shelf:** Cut a circular disc with an outer diameter of **17.5"**. In the center, cut a rectangular opening measuring **9.1" x 7.2"** to securely seat the AAXA M8 UST while leaving structural clearance for ports.

---

## 🏗️ Step-by-Step Mechanical Assembly

### Step 1: Building the Spinning Core
1. Take your **14-inch tall cut segment of 18-inch Sonotube**. 
2. Coat the outer rim of your **17.5-inch Plywood Base Plug** with wood glue and push it into the bottom edge of the Sonotube until it sits perfectly flush. Drive 1-5/8" screws from the outside of the cardboard directly into the plywood rim every 3 inches to lock it permanently.
3. Measure **9.3 inches down** from the top edge of the Sonotube. Secure three internal scrap wood cleats at this depth, and anchor your **1/2-inch Projector Mounting Shelf** to them using 1-1/4" screws. This locks the shelf precisely in place to respect the required **2.2-inch throw gap** between the top of the projector lens and your workspace surface.

### Step 2: Setting up the Rigid Base
1. Center the **16-inch Besimple Lazy Susan Bearing** over the cutout on your **22" Square Static Desk Base Plate**. Bolt the lower ring of the bearing securely using machine fasteners.
2. Flip your spinning Sonotube core upside down. Center the exposed top ring of the Lazy Susan bearing onto the plywood base plug sticking out of your tube, and screw it down firmly. 
3. Flip the assembly right-side up. Mount your **three 14-inch Outrigger Arms** vertically to the outside perimeter of the square base plate, spaced exactly 120 degrees apart. Bolt your inline skate wheels into the pre-drilled holes at the top of these arms. Adjust the arms so the wheels pinch tightly against the exterior face of the cardboard Sonotube.

### Step 3: Integrating the Drawing Interface
1. Set your **19.5-inch CNC-cut acrylic animation disc** over the top edge of the Sonotube. 
2. Align your **8.5" x 11" rear projection film window** directly with the path of the underlying projector shelf. Secure the acrylic disc to the top rim of the cardboard cylinder using structural block clamps or adhesive tape.
3. Secure your **AAXA M8 UST** horizontally on the inner 1/2-inch shelf, lock down its cable lines to the bracket using P-clips, and connect your flat extension line. 

Your structural light table is now completely isolated against radial drift and ready to smoothly rotate at a 45-degree drawing angle!
